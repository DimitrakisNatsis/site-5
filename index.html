Corporate Overview

ARMA TRADING 3339 LTD EOOD is a premier industrial supply enterprise based in Sofia, Bulgaria. Operating under the strategic direction of Key Principal Lyusien Teodosiev, we bridge the gap between heavy equipment manufacturers and industrial facilities across the European Union.

We are a dedicated B2B partner in the Wholesale Trade sector, specializing in the complex logistics and distribution of industrial machinery, heavy equipment, and commercial supplies. Our commitment is to ensure operational continuity for factories and construction firms through reliable, rapid wholesale supply chains.

ARMA Connect B2B Portal

To provide our industrial clients with unprecedented control over their supply chain, ARMA TRADING 3339 LTD EOOD has developed a proprietary B2B mobile application published on the Google Play Store.

The ARMA Connect app is a robust procurement tool. It enables factory managers and purchasing agents to browse interactive machinery catalogs, order heavy-duty spare parts via barcode scanning, and track the real-time logistics of oversized freight directly from their Android devices.

  • Digital B2B catalog for industrial machinery and durable goods.
  • Instant spare parts ordering via mobile barcode scanning.
  • Live GPS tracking for heavy equipment freight deliveries.
  • Secure access to AR (Augmented Reality) machinery maintenance manuals.
